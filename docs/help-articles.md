Written 24 Nov 2026 from the app's own screens. Button names are in **bold** exactly as they appear.
Collections: Getting started · Planning · Sharing · On your trip · Account and payment.

## Getting started

### 1. Plan your first journey

1. On your journeys screen, tap **Plan a journey**.
2. Fill in the name, the destination, your dates and who is travelling. You can add emails under **Invite** to bring people in now, or do it later.
3. Tap **Plan this journey**.

Your first journey is free.

Not ready yet? Tap the **Example** tile, "Paris and Normandy", to look around a finished 8-day journey. It is only made when you tap it, you can delete it any time, and it does not use up your free journey.

### 2. Add places and put them on a day

There are three ways to add a place:

- **On a day:** tap **Add a place** and search. You can search your saved places or anywhere.
- **On the map:** use the search box at the top of the map.
- **Find places:** suggests things to do and places to eat. Tap **+ Save** on the ones you like.

A saved place shows as a pin on the map. Open the pin and tap **Put on a day** to schedule it.

Short on time? Tap **Plan my trip**. Roam puts your saved places on your days, and you can change anything after.

### 3. Set a time, or move a stop

- Tap the time on a stop (or **Set a time**). Type a start and end, or use − and + to change the length. Tap **Done**.
- **Clear time** removes the time and keeps the stop on the day.

On a phone, a day is always in time order, so to move a stop earlier or later, change its time.

On a computer, you can also drag a stop on the week view: sideways to another day, up or down to another time, and drag its bottom edge to make it longer or shorter.

### 4. Use the map

- **Phone:** tap the map button on a day.
- **Computer:** the map sits beside your week. In a narrow window, use the **Map** button at the top.

Pin colours: green is an activity, purple is food, navy is travel and stays. A hollow pin is saved but not on a day yet. A filled pin is on a day. Use **Filter** to show only what you want.

The map needs a connection. It is blank when you are offline.

## Planning

### 5. Flights, stays and cars

Open the **⋯** menu and tap **Bookings**. You will see rows for **Flights**, **Stays** and **Car**.

- **Already booked?** Tap **Upload a confirmation** and choose the PDF or a photo of it. You can pick several at once. Roam reads them, shows you what it found, and you tap **Add to my days**.
- **Rather type it in?** Tap **Add one yourself**.
- Tap the mark on a row to set it to **Booked**, **Not needed** or **Not booked yet**, or to record what it cost.

When you add a stay, Roam adds a check-in and a check-out to the right days.

To add a drive, train, bus or ferry between two places, tap **Add a place** on the day and choose **Travel**.

### 6. Budget and notes

Both are in the **⋯** menu.

- **Budget** adds up what the journey will cost. You can add your own costs and note what was paid with points. Only the journey's owner sees the budget.
- **Notes** is a checklist for the journey: things to pack, things to book, things to remember. People you have invited can read it.

## Sharing

### 7. Share a journey with family and friends

Open the **⋯** menu and tap **Settings**. It opens at **Share**.

- **Invite by email:** type their email address and tap **Send**. They see each change as you make it.
- **Share a link:** tap **Make a link**, then **Copy link**. Anyone with the link can look, without an account.

What the people you invite can do: see the days, times and places, read the notes, and see the bookings. They cannot edit the journey, and they do not see your budget.

People who open the link without signing in see the days, times and places, but not your files, notes, budget or map.

To stop sharing, use **remove** beside a person, or **Revoke** on the link. Revoking the link does not remove people you invited by email.

## On your trip

### 8. Put Roam on your phone, and use it offline

Roam works like an app once it is on your home screen.

- **iPhone:** open Roam in Safari, tap **⋯**, then **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** tap **Add to phone** when Roam offers it.

Offline: every day of an upcoming journey is saved on your phone, along with any day you have opened. You can read your plan with no signal. The map and photos need a connection.

If you change something while offline, Roam saves it when you are back online. A small note on screen tells you if a change has not saved yet.

### 9. Save a place from TikTok or Instagram (Android)

Once Roam is on your Android phone's home screen:

- **TikTok:** tap Share, then More, then **Roam**.
- **Instagram:** tap the paper plane, then Share to, then **Roam**.

Roam asks which place it is, then adds it to a journey nearby, a journey you pick, or your wishlist.

This is not available on iPhone yet. On an iPhone, search for the place in Roam instead.

## Account and payment

### 10. What Roam costs

- Your first journey is free.
- After that, it is **$5 (Canadian), once**. That unlocks unlimited journeys. There is no subscription.
- You will be asked when you plan a second journey, or copy a journey to new dates.
- Payment is taken on Stripe's secure checkout page. Roam never sees your card number.

The example journey does not count as your free one.

Something wrong with a payment? Open **Help** and send us a message.

### 11. Your account

- **Sign in** with Google, or ask for a sign-in link by email. There is no password to remember.
- **Profile** (your photo, top right) holds your home airport, home country, passport country and home address. Tap **Edit**, then **Save**. Only you see your home address.
- **Archive a journey** to put it away without losing it. **Delete** removes it for good; there is no undo.
- **Delete your account** from Profile. This deletes every journey, place, note and file of yours, and your sign-in. It cannot be undone. Journeys other people invited you to are not affected.

### 12. Get help, and find your past messages

Open the **⋯** menu and tap **Help**, or tap your photo and choose **Help and messages**.

- **Ask a question** starts a new message to us.
- **Your messages** shows every conversation you have had with us, including the answers.
- **Walkthroughs** and **How-to videos** show you how Roam works, step by step.

A small dot on Help means we have replied.
